## 2. WHAT THIS WEBSITE DOES

The Website is an informational portfolio. It presents our collections and atelier services through photographs, text and one embedded video. It has no user accounts, no shopping basket, no payments, no newsletter and no contact form. Any conversation with us starts when you choose to email or call us.

## 3. INFORMATION WE PROCESS

### 3.1 Information you send us

If you email or call us, we receive what you choose to share, typically your name, email address, phone number, measurements, photographs or sketches for a commission, and the content of your message. We use it only to answer you and, if you place an order, to make and deliver your garment.

### 3.2 Information collected automatically

The Website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like any web server, it records technical access data such as your IP address, browser type, the pages requested and the time of the request, for security and to keep the service running. GitHub's handling of this data is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement at https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement. We do not receive this data and do not run any analytics of our own.

### 3.3 Cookies and similar technologies

The Website itself sets no cookies, uses no analytics, no advertising tags and no local storage.

The collection page embeds one video through YouTube's privacy-enhanced player (youtube-nocookie.com). YouTube, a service of Google, sets no cookies until you play the video. Once you press play, Google may set cookies and process your IP address under its own privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy. If you prefer not to interact with Google, do not play the video.

The Website loads an icon font from the Cloudflare cdnjs service and a Markdown rendering library from jsDelivr. Those content delivery networks see your IP address in order to serve the file, as any web server does, and do not set cookies.

### 3.4 Links to social networks

The footer links to our Instagram, Facebook and TikTok profiles. These are plain links, not embedded widgets. Nothing is sent to those platforms until you follow a link, after which their own privacy policies apply.

## 4. WHY AND ON WHAT LEGAL BASIS

- Answering your enquiry and preparing a quotation: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Making, altering and delivering a garment you ordered: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
- Keeping invoices and order records: compliance with tax and accounting obligations (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).
- Technical hosting logs: the legitimate interest of the hosting provider and ourselves in a secure, functioning website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
- Playing the embedded video: your consent, given by pressing play (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

## 5. HOW LONG WE KEEP INFORMATION

We keep correspondence for as long as needed to handle your enquiry or order and for a reasonable period afterwards in case you come back for alterations or a related request. Records that must be kept for tax and accounting purposes are retained for the period required by law. Everything else is deleted when it is no longer needed.

## 6. WHO WE SHARE INFORMATION WITH

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with:

- our email provider, which stores and transmits messages you send us;
- the courier that delivers your order, who receives your name, address and phone number;
- GitHub, Google and the content delivery networks named above, to the limited extent described in Section 3;
- public authorities, where the law requires it.

## 7. INTERNATIONAL TRANSFERS

GitHub, Google, Cloudflare and jsDelivr operate servers outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. These providers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission to protect data transferred out of the EEA.

## 8. YOUR RIGHTS

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you have the right to:

- access the personal information we hold about you;
- have inaccurate information corrected;
- have your information erased, where there is no legal reason to keep it;
- restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
- receive the information you gave us in a portable format;
- withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that took place before withdrawal;
- lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Our supervisory authority is the Bulgarian Commission for Personal Data Protection (https://cpdp.bg).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at ivanka.raykova@yahoo.com. We answer within one month.

## 9. CHILDREN

The Website is not aimed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. SECURITY

The Website is served over HTTPS only. We keep correspondence and order records in password-protected accounts and devices and share them with nobody beyond the recipients listed in Section 6. No transmission over the internet is entirely risk-free, so please do not send us more information than the request needs.
